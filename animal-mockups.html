Farm Animal Visual Mockups

32×64 pixel canvases (same footprint as enemies), scaled up. Three distinct art directions; each row shows Sheep, Cow, Pig.

Option A — Chunky side-view (Terraria-like)

Strong silhouettes, readable at 1× tile height. Side profile facing right; bold legs and snout/head shapes.

Option B — Soft & round (friendly Mobs)

Big rounded bodies, minimal outline, cute proportions — closer to plush toys than realism.

Option C — Compact & textured (wool spots / hide)

Slightly smaller on the canvas with extra surface detail: wool tufts, cow patches, pig bristle dots.
